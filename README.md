## 📝 Project Description

2D platformer video game developed with the Unity engine by a team of 6 students using the Kanban agile methodology (Trello).

> [!Note]
> Due to the time constraints of the course, current development focuses exclusively on one biome (the desert) out of the four originally planned for the full narrative. This version represents a functional **Vertical Slice** that demonstrates the mechanics, art, and complete game loop within this first environment, serving as a proof of concept for the project's final vision.

## 📖 Story & Characters

The game follows the story of a small penguin peacefully napping on its back in the sea, when suddenly the tide sweeps it far from home and strands it in the middle of an arid desert. Upon waking up, the penguin realizes it is lost, and sets off on a journey full of dangers and challenges to find its way back to the cold of the Arctic.

Along the way, the penguin must face different environments, enemies, and traps, tackling levels that will test the player's wit and reflexes.
